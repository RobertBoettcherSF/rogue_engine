# Containers & weight

- **Load** = Σ (hull mass + content mass)
- **Content state:** Solid · Liquid · Gas · Plasma
- **Hull integrity:** 0–100% (100 sealed, ≤50 opened, 0 ruptured) — not actor HP
- **Tools:** can opener (controlled open to access threshold); drill sample (solid); process sample
- **Planned:** siphon liquid; valve bleed gas
- **Plasma rupture:** heat spike + burn + electrocute effects, then containment lost (game over)
- **Carry:** Strength × 1 kg comfortable; 2× hard cap (e.g. Str 5 → 5 / 10 kg)
