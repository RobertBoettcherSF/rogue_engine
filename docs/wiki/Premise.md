# Premise

Two linked play spaces, one MIT Ada sim:

## Human (bunker)

- Location: sealed shelter **4 floors** below surface
- Vitality: **tissue oxygenation** (0–100%), driven by small-room air (O₂ / CO₂ / pressure)
- Ops: headset at a **central console island** in the player room
- Does not walk the storm map as the primary avatar

## Robot / rover (outside)

- Location: Earth dust storm with Mars-like hostility
- Vitality: **power / hull / thermal** (no lungs)
- Outside conditions: pressure crashed, cold, visibility ~0, dark though radio may say midday, aurora over the dust
- Coarse guidance from a **high satellite** still (ground cam often blind)

## Link

- **Sealed double-door airlock** — never both doors open; chamber pressure cycles bunker ↔ storm before the matching door opens
